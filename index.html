Web Serial Firmware Flasher

This tool flashes the firmware directly from the browser to your ESP32-C3 OLO Bit device. Ensure the firmware/ directory contains: bootloader.bin, partitions.bin, and firmware.bin.

  • 1. Bootloader (bootloader.bin) Address: 0x0
  • 2. Partition Table (partitions.bin) Address: 0x8000
  • 3. App Firmware (firmware.bin) Address: 0x10000
